About

Curiosity should lead somewhere.

The world is full of interesting people, claims, practices, places, and things to make or try. Opinions about them are easy to find. The material behind those opinions is harder. We make that part easier to reach, and leave the conclusion to you.

One idea, three steps

Look. Learn. Find.

01 / Look

Something catches your eye.

A short video, kept by a reader or an editor with a note on why it stayed. Clips live in the Look Ledger. A clip is a reason to look, not a verdict.

02 / Learn

We open the file.

A reported story asks the real question, then shows what the sources support, what others argue, and what remains open. Numbered footnotes open the original record.

03 / Find

Somewhere to take it next.

A person, place, guide, or product that holds up, with the criteria we used and any commercial relationship stated beside it.

These three are kept visibly separate. A saved clip, a reported story, and a recommended Find do not carry the same authority, and we label which is which. They meet on thread pages.
