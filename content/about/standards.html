Standards

How we work.

Four kinds of thing appear on this site. They are labeled, because they do not carry the same weight.

A saved video

A reader or editor kept it, with a note on why. The label says Reader-added or Editor-added. Neither label means the video is true, checked, or endorsed. The original creator and the person who saved it are always shown separately. Ledger entries carry an index like L / 042.

A reported story

A Learn story says what question it asked, what was checked, what the sources support, what other views exist, and what remains open. Claims that can be traced carry a numbered footnote (SOURCE 01) to the original record, with its title, publisher or author, date, and why it matters.

A real-world Find

A Find is a judgment, and it shows its criteria: why it made the list, what we checked, limits, and what to know before you go, try, or buy. Any commercial relationship is stated on the Find itself. "Best" always comes with the criteria.

A thread

A thread is one subject followed across all three. Thread pages keep the shelves apart: what people are saving, what the record shows, and where to try it. Editors create and merge threads; anyone signed in can propose one.

Firsthand experience

When someone reports trying something, we present it as their experience and say what, if anything, we verified.

Corrections

Corrections and updates stay visible on the page and in the corrections log.

Contributions

You can send a question, a source, a video, or a report. We credit contributors, review claims before elevating them, and explain corrections. Public saves are opt-in; private saves stay private.
