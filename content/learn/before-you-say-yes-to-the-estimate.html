An estimate is a number attached to a set of assumptions. The number gets the attention; the assumptions decide what you actually pay.[[s:1]]

Ask what is, and is not, in the number

Two quotes for the "same" job can describe different jobs. One includes removing old material and cleanup. The other does not. Write the scope down, line by line, before you compare prices.[[find:a-written-scope-checklist]]

Ask how changes get handled

Walls and floors sometimes hide surprises. A good quote says what happens then: who decides, who pays, and how it is recorded.[[s:2]]
