Two signals that point different ways

New Jersey's 7th Congressional District spans Warren and Hunterdon counties and parts of Morris, Somerset, Sussex and Union. WHYY reports it has about 17,000 more registered Republicans than Democrats.[[s:2]] The Philadelphia Inquirer reports that three forecasters, including Cook Political Report, rate the contest as leaning Democratic.[[s:1]]

Those two facts measure different things. Registration counts how voters enrolled. A forecaster's rating is a judgment about the race. Neither says how any voter will vote, and we do not make a prediction here.

The challenger and the incumbent

Republican Rep. Tom Kean Jr. is the incumbent. Democrat Rebecca Bennett, 39, is a former Navy helicopter pilot and health technology executive, the Inquirer reports. The same report says Kean returned in June after a nearly four-month medical leave, and that two major Republican PACs pulled funding from the race.[[s:1]] The report does not give amounts or dates for the PAC decisions, so we do not measure their effect. An independent, Seamus Patrick O'Toole, is also on the ballot.[[s:2]][[s:6]]

Two fundraising figures that do not line up

According to the Inquirer, Kean raised $4.89 million through June 30, a figure it attributes to FEC filings. Bennett announced $4 million raised since July 1, a figure that comes from her campaign.[[s:1]]
