The money

In the New Jersey Senate race, Democratic Sen. Cory Booker reported $22,899,410.72 in cash on hand through October 14. Republican Justin Murphy reported $10,190.62, according to OpenFEC candidate totals from each campaign's pre-general report.[[s:1]]

Receipts and cash on hand are different numbers. For the 2026 cycle Booker reported receipts of $21,275,162.52 and Murphy $68,188.87.[[s:1]] These are reported figures, not audited by LLF.

The debate answer

Fox News reported on October 19 that Murphy, a Navy veteran, said President Trump should "finish it" in the Iran war during a debate with Booker. The outlet says Booker appeared stunned and that Murphy said he had not expected the exchange to go viral.[[s:2]] Booker's campaign did not comment for that report, and the article does not give the debate's date. We have no audience figures for the clip, so we cannot say how far it traveled. We also have no evidence that it moved any voter or any price.

The ballot

The Division of Elections' official Senate list, dated July 27, 2026, and WHYY's September 22 guide both list four candidates: Booker, Murphy, Veronica Fernandez of End the Corruption! and Joanne Kuniansky of the Socialist Workers Party.[[s:3]][[s:8]] The Green Papers also lists a write-in, Eduardo Jose Castillo Nicasio, but the official list does not include him.[[s:7]][[s:8]]
