The race at a glance

The 17th Congressional District covers all of Putnam and Rockland counties and parts of Dutchess and Westchester. Mike Lawler, a Republican, flipped the seat in 2022 and is seeking re-election. His Democratic challenger is Cait Conley, an Army veteran who has never been a legislator. City & State reports the district has about 78,000 more registered Democrats than Republicans, and Kamala Harris carried it 49.9% to 49.3% in 2024.

One poll, inside the margin of error

Emerson College Polling surveyed 400 likely voters in the district for PIX11 on September 27 to 29. Conley had 48%, Lawler 46% and 7% were undecided. The credibility interval is ±4.9 points, so the two are within the margin. In the same poll Lawler was 45% favorable and 46% unfavorable, and Conley was 44% favorable, 35% unfavorable and 22% neutral.

We found no public poll newer than September 29 in our search. A single poll of 400 people is a snapshot, not a forecast.
