A tag on a shelf is the end of a chain: growing, packing, shipping, storing, staffing, rent.[[s:1]] Each step has a cost, and each has someone making a decision.

Follow one item

Pick one thing you buy every week and ask what had to happen for it to be on that shelf. You will not get every number, but you will learn which questions have answers.[[s:2]]

Ask the person at the counter

Small shops often know the parts of the chain they control, and will say so.[[find:talk-to-your-local-grocer]]
