Privacy

Your privacy.

Draft. Have this page reviewed before launch.

Accounts. If you create a profile we store your email, a hashed password, your chosen handle and display name, and what you save. We do not sell this.

Private by default. Saves are private unless you deliberately add one to the public Ledger or a public collection. Private saves and notes are never shown on a profile or in anyone's Following feed. Profiles can be kept entirely private. You can export your saves at any time.

Newsletter and forms. Emails you give us for the newsletter, contributions, or contact are used for that purpose only.

Third parties. Videos are not hosted here; links open the original platform, and thumbnails load from that platform.
