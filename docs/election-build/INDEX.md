## Illinois State of the Race pages (built 2026-10-28; local only, not deployed)

Illinois differences by design: no FEC totals or outside-spending figures on the pages (links to the FEC pages instead), no polls (none with an original release), a visible roster limitation on every state-level page (State Board list HTTP 403; roster is not official or complete), Kalshi's still-listed contract for Mayra Macías (removed from the IL-04 ballot) is flagged. **Directory gaps:** 11 listed candidates have no TIP profile; the build reports them as "DIRECTORY GAPS" (non-blocking) instead of R1 violations, and they must be resolved on TIP, or the owner must decide, before any Illinois page is published.
