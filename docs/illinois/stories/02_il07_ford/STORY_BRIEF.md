## The one-sentence story
In the open 7th District primary, FEC filings show the United Democracy Project, which AdImpact calls an AIPAC-aligned PAC, spent about $4.97 million supporting Melissa Conyears-Ervin; La Shawn Ford finished first with about 24 percent to her 20.

## Central conflict and the surprising insight
**Conflict:** the largest documented outside spender in the race backed the runner-up while a crypto PAC attacked the winner. **Insight:** two outside groups put about $6.6 million into the race (UDP $4.7M for Conyears-Ervin and Fairshake $1.9M against Ford, per AdImpact) and the candidate they spent against won with a plurality in a field of 13. Keep documented spending (FEC) apart from described ties ("AIPAC-aligned" is AdImpact's and the Tribune's wording; FEC data identifies a committee by name and ID only).

## Verified synopsis
Ford, a state representative, finished first in the Democratic primary for the seat Danny Davis is leaving, with about 24 percent in unofficial returns to Conyears-Ervin's roughly 20 and Driver's roughly 11. The AP projected the result and Conyears-Ervin conceded (per a search summary of WTTW; confirm). Davis backed Ford, the Tribune reported.

The United Democracy Project supported Conyears-Ervin. LLF's de-duplicated count of its FEC filings is $4,968,500 between February 10 and March 10; AdImpact put its spending at $4.7 million of her $5.5 million as of March 13. Fairshake, a crypto PAC, spent about $1.9 million attacking Ford by AdImpact's count. Ford faces Republican Chad Koppie Sr. in November in a heavily Democratic district.

## Responses
Ford called the Fairshake ads slanderous (Tribune, paraphrase). Conyears-Ervin's quoted comments concern her record, not outside money. No AIPAC or UDP statement was found; ask for one before publishing and say if none comes.
