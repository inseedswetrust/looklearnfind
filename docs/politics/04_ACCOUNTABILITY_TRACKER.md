# 04 — Accountability Desk: 2027 congressional leadership

## Editorial position and separation
The newly elected Congress's leadership choices are timely subjects of political reporting. A campaign demanding different party leaders belongs to The Issues Party Action Party, distinctly labeled as advocacy. LLF's public tracker must remain a factual, source-first record and must not convert absence of a response into opposition or assert that election outcomes prove voters endorsed a specific leadership replacement.

## Proposed neutral reporting title
`Who Will Lead the New Congress?` — `Leadership positions, public statements and unanswered questions`.

## Scope
Track public statements about House Democratic leadership (including Hakeem Jeffries), Senate Democratic leadership (including Chuck Schumer), named leadership challengers and actual caucus decisions. Include documented statements from incumbent and incoming members where available, separately identifying members-elect from sworn members. Dates and process vary by chamber; verify any reported meeting or voting date before publishing. Public or secret-ballot caucus choice must not be inferred from public statements.

## Fields
`record_id`, `person_id`, `person_name`, `chamber`, `state`, `district`, `membership_status`, `leadership_question`, `position` (`supports_named_candidate`, `opposes_named_candidate`, `undecided_publicly`, `no_public_statement_found`, `declined_to_answer`, `not_applicable`), `statement_text_excerpt`, `source_url`, `source_type`, `statement_date`, `checked_at`, `verified_by`, `review_status`, `correction_history`, `tip_candidate_url`, `tip_action_party_url` (labeled external advocacy), `notes`.

## Important distinctions
- `No public statement found` is not `no answer`, and neither is a vote.
- `Declined to answer` is shown only with a documented contact/statement record.
- `Support/opposition` must refer to an exact named leadership selection and have a dated source.
- Statements cannot be reused as positions on unrelated issues.
- Do not display apparent vote counts unless the denominator, eligible voters and source are verified. A public-stance tally is not a caucus ballot tally.
- Do not publish private direct outreach records, contact details or personal data.

## TIP cross-links
On LLF: `View the public record at The Issues Party` -> exact candidate profile; an Action Party link clearly labeled external advocacy. TIP retains control of any participation, outreach, consent and activity reporting. No LLF-to-TIP write without explicit approval and auditability.

## Verification
Two-person editorial review of first update and later changed positions; provide source-as-of time, append-only statement history and a public correction mechanism. No endorsed response wording or legislator-specific persuasion copy is included in this reporting package.
