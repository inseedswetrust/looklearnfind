## Production directions for the candidate cutout

1. Fetch the verified original Jeffries and Schumer photos and preserve originals unaltered.
2. Remove backgrounds by masking **without synthesizing faces**, clothing or significant facial features.
3. Desaturate to natural grayscale with readable midtones, no exaggerated contrast that changes features.
4. Apply narrow 4–8 px off-white paper reveal with slight edge irregularity, roughly consistent on both subjects.
5. Layer Jeffries and Schumer over a separately sourced grayscale Capitol/chamber environment, with subtle overlap but no invented meeting context.
6. Add a muted teal directional geometric shape **behind** the portraits, not over their eyes, mouths or source labels.
7. Export layered originals plus a flattenable WebP/JPEG for the site. Keep text HTML/CSS where this is a website module.
8. Caption as a *photo illustration assembled from separate original portraits*; do not suggest the photo depicts one shared event.
