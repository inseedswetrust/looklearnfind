How to read results here

Leading means a candidate has the most counted votes at the time shown. Called means a named organization declared a winner at a stated time. Certified means the responsible authority has certified the count. Each is shown separately, with its source.

Where results come from

Each number carries its source, the time we retrieved it and the time the source says it was updated. Where a source cannot be read by our tools we say so and show nothing in its place.
