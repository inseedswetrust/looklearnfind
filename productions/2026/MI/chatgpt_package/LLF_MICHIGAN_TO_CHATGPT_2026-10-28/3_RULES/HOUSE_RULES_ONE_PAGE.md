# House rules (one page)

- Scope: Governor, U.S. Senate, U.S. House only. No ballot measures, legislature, judges or Cabinet offices.
- No Oxford comma anywhere.
- Neutral: no win probabilities, no rankings, no endorsements, no causal claims linking spending, polls or markets to outcomes.
- Every number: source, date, unit, period. Never "audited" or "official" unless the source is the official record.
- Kalshi: exact contract, price side, UTC timestamp. A price is not a forecast.
- Polls: field dates, sponsor, sample, method, margin of error, question wording. Do not give numbers the release does not give.
- TIP grades: cite a grade only if it is visible on the TIP profile you link. "Not graded" is never zero. These two stories use none.
- Links: first mention of each named candidate to their TIP profile; first mention of each race to the race page.
- Financial reporting (not needed in these two stories): keep donations, receipts, independent expenditures and ad-tracking separate.
- The drafter does not fact-check their own article.
- Nothing is published without the owner's approval; approval for one item does not cover another.
- Hero images are AI illustrations with a disclosure credit; no AI portraits of real candidates; no synthetic footage of real people or events.
