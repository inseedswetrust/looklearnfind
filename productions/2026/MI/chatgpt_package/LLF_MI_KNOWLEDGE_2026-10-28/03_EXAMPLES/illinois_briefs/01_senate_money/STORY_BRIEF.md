## The one-sentence story
Raja Krishnamoorthi's campaign accounted for about $28.9 million of the ad money in Illinois' Senate primary and Juliana Stratton's campaign for about $1.1 million; Stratton won, with a Pritzker-backed PAC adding $14.4 million for her and a crypto PAC $9.0 million against her.

## Central conflict and the surprising insight
**Conflict:** a self-funded front-runner with a long head start against a lieutenant governor whose campaign was nearly broke but whose backer controlled a PAC. **Insight:** the winner's own campaign was outspent about 26 to 1 on ads (AdImpact), yet the race was decided in the gap between *candidate* money and *outside* money: roughly half of Stratton's $15.5 million came from a PAC the governor backed, and the largest negative spender was a crypto PAC, not a party group. Do not say what the money did; say who paid for what, and let the result stand beside it.

## Verified synopsis (two paragraphs)
AdImpact counted $55.3 million in Senate primary ad spending and reservations as of March 13. Krishnamoorthi's $28.9 million came almost entirely from his campaign, which Capitol News Illinois says began the race with more than $19 million in the bank. Stratton's $15.5 million was $1.1 million from her campaign and $14.4 million from the Pritzker-backed Illinois Future PAC. Fairshake, a crypto-focused PAC, spent $9.0 million attacking her by AdImpact's count; The Prospect says almost $10 million and the Sun-Times counted more than $5.5 million in ads, so the total depends on the outlet.

Stratton won with 39.6 percent to Krishnamoorthi's 33.6 at 82 percent of precincts; Robin Kelly had 18.5. Krishnamoorthi conceded before the AP call. Stratton faces Republican Don Tracy, the former state party chair, on November 3. We found no general-election poll.

## Responses and competing explanations
Krishnamoorthi conceded and said he would not question the outcome (CNI). Stratton told reporters she had not accepted money from the AIPAC PAC and did not say whether she would take money from an affiliated group (Prospect, pre-primary). Pritzker's role is described two ways: "largely bankrolled" the Illinois Future PAC (CNI) and "at least $5 million of his own money" toward her campaign (Prospect). Quote both.
