## The one-sentence story
Rep. Jesús "Chuy" García filed to run for a new term, then withdrew; his chief of staff Patty García (no relation) filed just before the deadline, was the only Democrat in the primary, and two independents who might have challenged her were later removed from the ballot for falling short on signatures.

## Verified synopsis
García filed petitions for another term on Oct. 27, 2025. His chief of staff, Patty García, who is not related to him, filed shortly before the Nov. 3 deadline, and he later withdrew his own. He said his cardiologist advised him to step aside and his wife is fighting MS, and he rejected the idea that he was anointing a successor; critics called it a coronation. She was the only Democrat on the primary ballot.

Independents Sigcho-Lopez and Macías, who needed 10,816 valid signatures to her 697, were removed by the State Board of Elections in an 8-0 vote on July 21, after hearing officers found them 1,222 and 486 signatures short; supporters of Patty García had challenged the signatures. Sigcho-Lopez sued in August; we found no ruling. The ballot lists García, Castillo, Getty and Hershey.

## Responses
Chuy García: family reasons, not the machine; anyone could have filed (ABC7, Nov 2025). Macías and Sigcho-Lopez: "disenfranchisement" and "rigged" (RB Landmark). Patty García's campaign: no response to the removals appears in the sources; ask for one. The Board: does not comment on pending litigation.
