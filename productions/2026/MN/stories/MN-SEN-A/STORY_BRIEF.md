## MN-SEN-A: Minnesota Senate: a four-point poll lead within the margin, a market near 0.91 and a race that has stayed quiet (READY)
MPR News (Oct 12) reports the Senate race to replace Tina Smith has drawn little outside attention compared with Texas, Michigan and Kansas, with few news conferences or big rallies. It cites a Star Tribune/KARE 11 poll published that Monday (fielded the prior week, margin of error 3.5 points) with Flanagan at 47%, Tafoya at 43% and about 9% undecided, and says outside groups spent more than $20 million in the primary but only about $2 million since, about $1.3 million of it on pro-Flanagan billboards. Sen. John Thune told MPR there is 'more interest' without committing Senate Leadership Fund money. KTTC (Oct 16) covers the only scheduled debate: Flanagan tied Tafoya to President Trump, Tafoya tied Flanagan to Gov. Tim Walz and fraud cases and called herself a pro-choice Republican. Kalshi's Senate event (SENATEMN-26), captured Oct 31 19:51 UTC, shows the Democratic-party contract at 0.908 last (bid 0.907, ask 0.908) and the Republican-party contract at 0.096 last (bid 0.094, ask 0.096). Neither candidate has a congressional record and neither is graded on TIP. A KSTP/SurveyUSA poll (45% to 42%, margin 4.2 points) is reported by Political Wire but its original was not opened and it is not used. The Star Tribune original returned an error and was not opened, so the poll is attributed to MPR's report.
