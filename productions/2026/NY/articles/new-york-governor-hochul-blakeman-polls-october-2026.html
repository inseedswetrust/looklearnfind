The race at a glance

New York elects a governor on November 3. Kathy Hochul, the Democratic incumbent, faces Bruce Blakeman, the Republican Nassau County Executive. This is the New York governor's race on TIP. Two late-October polls have Hochul ahead by double digits, and Kalshi's contract on a Democratic win last traded near 96 cents. None of those is a forecast.

What the two polls found

Siena surveyed 807 likely voters October 19 to 22 (margin of error ±4.1). Hochul had 54%, Blakeman 40% and 6% were undecided. In Siena's September poll Hochul led by nine points, so the gap grew by five. City & State, which reported the poll on October 28, says Blakeman led independents 44% to 39% in September and Hochul now leads them 51% to 40%. Siena says the two are running about even upstate and in the downstate suburbs.

Marist polled 1,448 registered voters October 22 to 25. Among likely voters (margin of error ±3.9) it has Hochul at 53%, Blakeman at 42% and 4% undecided. Among registered voters Marist reports a 14-point Hochul lead. The Marist page does not name a sponsor.

What the market says

Kalshi's New York governor contracts resolve on which party's candidate is inaugurated, and are labeled with the candidates' names. Captured at 17:57 UTC on October 30, the Democratic-party contract (labeled Hochul) last traded at 0.955, with a bid of 0.956 and an ask of 0.957. The Republican-party contract (labeled Blakeman) last traded at 0.044, with a bid of 0.042 and an ask of 0.044. A market price is what traders were willing to pay for a $1 contract at that moment. It is not a poll, a vote share or a forecast.

Where the candidates stand

Governor is a state office, and TIP's six-issue grades rest on recorded congressional acts (how we grade). Neither Hochul nor Blakeman has a graded issue on TIP. Not graded is not a score of zero.
