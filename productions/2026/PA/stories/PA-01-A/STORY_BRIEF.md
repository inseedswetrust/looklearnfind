## PA-01-A: Pennsylvania's 1st: outside money arrives for Fitzpatrick, and two reports give different Congressional Leadership Fund figures (READY)
The Inquirer (Oct 16) reports the Congressional Leadership Fund redirected $3.6 million to Rep. Brian Fitzpatrick in his race against Democrat Bob Harvie, the DCCC began a nearly $1 million campaign for Harvie, and Fitzpatrick spent $5 million over the summer while Harvie raised about $888,000. Jewish Insider (Oct 17) puts CLF spending at $4.5 million that week, its first in the district since 2020, and adds a $400,000 Republican Jewish Coalition digital buy; it calls the seat one of two that Kamala Harris won in 2024 and a Republican holds, rated lean Republican by Cook. The figures differ and neither outlet explains why, so both are given with attribution and not added. Fitzpatrick has TIP grades (A+ Stock Trading Ban and Universal Healthcare; F- Data Bill of Rights, War-Crime Accountability and US-Israel Military Merger); Harvie is not graded.
